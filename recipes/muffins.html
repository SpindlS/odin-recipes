Banana Oat Muffins

Description

 These banana oat muffins are absolutely delicious and lean on the healthier side. The original recipe can be found here. This recipe's ingredients list an additonal cup of sugar and just 2 bananas, while the original does not include the additional sugar and calls for 3 bananas. I chose this version of the recipe as it is what I enjoy best and this version doesn't have too much overpowering banana flavor. Choosing overripe bananas is important to obtaining a good texture.

 

Ingredients

  • 200g or 1 cup brown sugar
  • 200g or 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 110g or 1/2 cup avocado oil (or neutral tasting oil)
  • 1 large egg white
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 overripe bananas mashed - about 2/3 cup or so and so grams
  • 90g or 1 cup oats
  • 120mL or 1/2 cup of water or milk of preference
  • 300g or 2 cups all purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • optional add-ins include chocolate chips and walnuts
