Banana Oat Muffins

Description

 These banana oat muffins are absolutely delicious and lean on the healthier side. The original recipe can be found here. This recipe's ingredients list an additonal cup of sugar and just 2 bananas, while the original does not include the additional sugar and calls for 3 bananas. I chose this version of the recipe as it is what I enjoy best and this version doesn't have too much overpowering banana flavor. Choosing overripe bananas is important to obtaining a soft and tender muffin.

 Optional add-ins for this recipe include chocolate chips, and walnuts. I highly recommend adding chocolate chips if you have any as they pair very well with the banana and oats. In the original recipe it uses rolled oats, I prefer and would recommend using quick oats instead. Quick oats tends to result in a softer more tender bake; while rolled oats tend to not absorb as much liquid as quickly so they sometimes cause a tougher and or more dense texture.

Ingredients

  • 200g or 1 cup brown sugar
  • 200g or 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 110g or 1/2 cup avocado oil (or neutral tasting oil)
  • 1 large egg white
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 overripe bananas mashed or about 1 cup
  • 90g or 1 cup oats
  • 120mL or 1/2 cup of water or milk of preference
  • 300g or 2 cups all purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • optional add-ins include chocolate chips and walnuts

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 350F and line a 12 cup muffin tin.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the sugars, oil, egg whites, eggs, and vanilla.
  3. In a separate bowl, mix the mashed bananas with the oats, water or milk, and then combine with the sugar mixture and combine well
  4. Mix together your flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon and mix. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix well.
  5. Pour the muffin batter into the muffin tins evenly.
  6. Bake muffins for 24-30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

Congrats! Your banana oat muffins are finished and ready to be enjoyed.
